Kitchen Wisdom

How to Preserve Natural Harvest Freshness

Practical tips from our agricultural team to keep your organic foods crisp and nutrient-packed.

Leafy Greens Preservation Protocol

Wrap in damp unbleached cloth: Place spinach and herbs in breathable cloth before refrigerating to prevent wilting.
Do not pre-wash: Moisture on leaves accelerates oxidation; wash only right before cooking or preparing salads.
Crispness restoration: Immerse limp celery or carrots in ice-cold water for 15 minutes to restore natural cell turgidity.

Orchard Fruit Care Guide

Avocados: Store hard avocados at room temperature; move to refrigerator crisper drawer once slightly yielding.
Berries: Keep strawberries in their ventilated wooden/cardboard box and avoid stacking heavy items on top.
Bananas & Apples: Store bananas separately as natural ethylene emissions ripen neighboring fruits faster.

Raw Unfiltered Honey Stewardship

Never refrigerate: Cold temperatures accelerate harmless natural honey crystallization; store in a dry pantry.
Gentle warm bath: If honey crystallizes, place glass jar in warm water (under 45°C) to preserve active live enzymes.
Dry wooden spoon: Avoid introducing water droplets inside the jar to keep antibacterial properties active.

Cold-Pressed Oil Potency Protection

Shield from direct sunlight: UV rays degrade sensitive polyphenol antioxidants; keep bottles in a dark pantry.
Tightly seal cap: Prevent oxidation by ensuring bottles are hermetically capped after each cooking use.
Virgin coconut oil: Natural solidifying below 24°C is a pure quality hallmark and does not affect quality.
